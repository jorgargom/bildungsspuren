About me

Education, learning and personal development. Experiences that have shaped my professional path and new perspectives in Germany.

Education, learning and personal development

My professional path has been connected with education, pedagogy and the development of learning programmes for many years.

I have worked in different countries and professional settings, gaining experience in educational coordination, pedagogical consultancy, training and professional development, team coordination and digital education.

I am interested in how educational programmes are created, how people learn and how supportive pedagogical environments can contribute to personal and professional development.

My professional background

Education management and pedagogical consultancy

In Equatorial Guinea, I worked as an educational adviser, contributing to the development and implementation of a new regulated vocational education model for the Ministry of Education and Science.

My responsibilities included developing pedagogical and curriculum concepts, planning educational structures, contributing to proposals for the development of education legislation, and creating educational content and digital administration tools.

I was also involved in organising and supporting educational institutions. Another area of work involved preparing and training new teachers in Spain and Equatorial Guinea, as well as providing pedagogical and technical support to a pilot centre in Bata.

This work gave me insight into the planning, organisation and development of vocational education.

Pedagogical coordination and educational work

At Nativo Digital – Talent School in Spain, I worked in educational coordination and pedagogical support.

I worked with children, young people and teachers. My responsibilities included organising and supporting educational activities, developing learning content and supporting pedagogical processes.

Working with different groups strengthened my interest in individual learning paths and respectful pedagogical support.

Digital education and learning platforms

Through my freelance work for Nextlane and Alumne LMS, I worked with digital education and learning platforms.

My work included designing and creating digital learning content, developing learning environments and managing learning management systems (LMS) from a technical perspective.

This work led me to explore how digital technologies can support education and make learning content more accessible and easier to understand.

Further professional experience

My professional background also includes work in occupational safety and risk prevention, as well as my current role in logistics in Germany.

These experiences have broadened my perspective on organisation, responsibility, teamwork and professional development.

Further training

My professional development includes further training in several areas of education, including:

  • Education management and leadership of educational institutions
  • Trainer training and learning process planning
  • Diversity and inclusive education
  • Pedagogical guidance and learner support
  • Communication, conflict resolution and mediation
  • Teaching resources and instructional design
  • First aid for children in educational and care settings

I have also explored educational organisation, adult learning and digital education.

My path in Germany

I now live in Germany and am building a new personal and professional chapter.

Learning German, getting to know the German education system and exploring new professional possibilities are part of my everyday life.

I want to connect my previous experience with new knowledge and continue developing, particularly in pedagogy, education, inclusion and educational work.

Why Bildungsspuren?

Bildungsspuren grew out of my personal interest in education and my own path of learning and development.

Here I want to record experiences, ask questions and share thoughts about education, language, pedagogy and personal development.

I do not see myself as someone who already has all the answers, but as someone who reflects on past experiences while remaining open to what comes next.

Education accompanies us throughout our lives. And every path leaves its traces.

Other projects

Alongside Bildungsspuren, I am developing another personal project, Mente de Equipaje. It explores human experiences, life changes, identity, personal decisions and the stories that shape us.

The two projects have different focuses, but come from the same personal curiosity: understanding people, their experiences and their development.
