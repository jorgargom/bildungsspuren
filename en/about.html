A personal path

About me

What has shaped me, why I work in education today and where I hope to grow next.

A path shaped by experience

I'm Jorge García Gómez. My path has taken me through different professional fields, countries and stages of life. Each experience has broadened the way I think about people, work and personal development.

Education has been part of many stages of my working life: developing training programmes, providing pedagogical support, creating digital learning content and working with learning platforms. These experiences have shaped my interest in education.

Why I want to keep developing professionally

Throughout my career, I have experienced different working environments. Over time, I have increasingly wanted to work more directly with people.

I know workplaces where numbers, performance indicators and targets shape everyday work. I have realised that I want a role where relationships, attention and personal development have more room.

I want to support people, accompany learning processes and contribute to their development. I know educational work also brings challenges and organisational demands. Still, I see it as a way to connect my experience with work that feels meaningful to me.

A new chapter in Germany

I now live in Germany and work as a pedagogical assistant in an early childhood setting. In everyday educational work, I am learning new ways of working, gaining practical experience and continuing to develop my German.

This stage allows me to experience education at close quarters, through daily interactions with children and collaboration with early years practitioners.

What interests me

I'm interested in education, pedagogy, lifelong learning, language, inclusion and human development. I want to understand how people learn and what helps them grow.

A path that continues

I want to keep learning, gain new experiences and develop professionally in education. I don't yet know exactly where this path will lead. Bildungsspuren is where I record that process and share the thoughts that accompany me.

I don't write as someone who has all the answers, but as someone who brings experience and remains open to learning.
