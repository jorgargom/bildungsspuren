01 · About me

A path shaped by experience.

I'm Jorge García Gómez. My professional path has taken me through different fields, from vocational education to digital learning. Along the way, my interest in how people learn, develop and find their own path has grown.

I now live in Germany and work as a pedagogical assistant in an early childhood setting. In my work, I'm particularly interested in the relationships that shape everyday life and the many small steps through which people learn and develop.
