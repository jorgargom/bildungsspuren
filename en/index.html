01 · About me

A path that changes me and helps me grow.

I am a Spanish speaker living in Germany. Here, I have begun a new path of learning and personal development.

For me, learning a new language means much more than mastering vocabulary and grammar. It means discovering new perspectives, finding your way in everyday and professional life, and gradually finding your place.

Over time, my interest in education, upbringing and pedagogy has grown. I want to learn more about the German education system, discover new professional paths and continue developing personally and professionally.

Bildungsspuren is my personal space to document this path. Here I share experiences, thoughts, questions and what I learn along the way.

Education accompanies us throughout our lives. And every path leaves its traces.
