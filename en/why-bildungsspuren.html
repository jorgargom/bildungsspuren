My path · Bildungsspuren

Why I started Bildungsspuren

A personal space for experiences, questions and thoughts about what shapes us and what we learn throughout our lives.

Sometimes it is only once we are on our way that we realise how much we are learning. A new country, another language and new professional opportunities bring many questions. We have to find our bearings, question what we have always taken for granted and gradually find new paths.

I live in Germany and am on this kind of path of learning and development myself. Learning German is part of it, as is finding my way through everyday life, working life and the opportunities that open up here.

Over time, my interest in education, upbringing and pedagogy has grown. I want to understand how education is organised in Germany, which paths people can take and what learning means at different stages of life.

Why Bildungsspuren?

The name refers to the traces that education and experience leave in our lives. We are shaped by people, encounters and what we learn. At the same time, we leave traces in the lives of others.

For me, education therefore means more than school, vocational training or a qualification. It accompanies us in everyday life, through personal changes and in the decisions we make.

A space that grows with me

With Bildungsspuren, I want to document my path and share the thoughts that occupy me. I will write about language and learning, education and pedagogy, personal experiences in Germany and projects that grow out of these interests.

I want to ask questions, record experiences and keep learning. Not as someone who already has all the answers, but as someone who remains curious and consciously continues along their path.

This is not a finished project. It is a beginning. And I am curious to see what traces will emerge from it.
