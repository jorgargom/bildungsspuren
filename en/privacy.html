2. Hosting and server data

This website is delivered through GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes technical connection data such as your IP address, date and time, requested URL, and browser and device information. The purpose is to deliver the website and maintain its stability and security. The legal basis relied on by the website operator is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in operating a secure and functional website. GitHub is the recipient. Data is retained for as long as necessary for security and operation, subject to the provider's own rules; the website operator does not control the provider's internal deletion periods. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub stores and processes personal data in different locations, including the United States and other countries. For transfers from the EEA, GitHub states that it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for other restricted transfers.

3. Contact by email

If you email kontakt@bildungsspuren.de, the information you provide, including your email address, message and any attachments, is processed to handle and answer your enquiry. Messages are forwarded by Porkbun and received in Proton Mail. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in replying to messages voluntarily sent to us and managing correspondence. Data is deleted once the enquiry has been resolved and further retention is no longer needed for clarification or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Statutory retention duties remain unaffected. The recipients are Porkbun and Proton. See the Porkbun Privacy Policy and Proton Mail Privacy Policy.

4. External content, cookies and analytics

Under the current configuration, this website does not embed external fonts, maps, videos, social-media content or analytics tools. It does not use analytics or marketing cookies or audience measurement. There is no contact form. External links, for example to Instagram, connect to those providers only after you click them.

5. Recipients and international transfers

The recipients are GitHub as hosting provider and Porkbun and Proton for email forwarding and receipt. GitHub also processes data in the United States and other countries. GitHub states that it uses, among other mechanisms, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area. Porkbun LLC is based in the United States. Its Data Processing Addendum provides for EU Standard Contractual Clauses for EEA data transferred through its services to countries without an adequacy decision, where that provision applies to the specific contractual relationship. Proton AG is based in Switzerland, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision. Actual processing locations and any onward transfers also depend on each provider's subprocessors. Further information: GitHub, Porkbun Data Processing Addendum, Proton Mail and the European Commission's adequacy decisions.

The competent authority in Bremen is the Landesbeauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Bremen, Georgstraße 122–124, 27570 Bremerhaven, email: office@datenschutz.bremen.de.
