Experience and development

My path

Professional experiences that have shaped me and continue to inform the path ahead.

Occupational safety and prevention

Construcciones Calderón

My early professional experience was in occupational safety and risk prevention. This introduced me to organisational processes, responsibility and structured teamwork.

Vocational education and pedagogical development

ICAF · Equatorial Guinea

At ICAF, I worked on the development and implementation of vocational education models, as well as pedagogical and curriculum concepts.

My work included developing learning content, supporting a pilot centre in Bata, and preparing and training teachers in Spain and Equatorial Guinea.

Educational coordination and pedagogical work

Nativo Digital – Talent School

At Nativo Digital, I worked in educational coordination and pedagogical support. I helped organise and support learning activities and worked with children, young people and teachers.

Developing learning content and supporting pedagogical processes were also part of this stage.

Digital education and learning platforms

Nextlane · Alumne LMS

Through my freelance work, I worked with digital learning content and learning platforms. This included planning and creating content, designing digital learning environments and technically managing learning management systems.

Pedagogical assistant in Germany

A new professional chapter

I now work as a pedagogical assistant in an early childhood setting. I am gaining practical experience, learning new ways of working and continuing to develop professionally and in German.

This stage connects my previous experience with direct work alongside people and learning processes.

What connects these experiences

Each stage has been different. Together, they have shaped my interest in how people learn, how collaboration works and how education can support personal and professional development.

My path is still unfolding.
